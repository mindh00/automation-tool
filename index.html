Automation tool

A private, self-hosted YouTube research and video-planning tool.

Automation tool runs on its owner's own computer. A channel owner signs in with Google to let the tool read that channel's own YouTube data (videos and YouTube Analytics reports) so it can show the channel's performance, research its niche, and plan the next videos.

Access is read-only. The tool never uploads, edits, deletes or posts anything on YouTube, and never shares the data with anyone.
